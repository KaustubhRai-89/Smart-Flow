About The Project

Our mission is to create an extensible AI framework that combines the power of large language models with computer vision, specifically tailored for analyzing ISRO's vast repository of Earth Observation data.

Advanced Language AI

Leveraging OpenAI's GPT Open Source Software (GPT-OSS) for its powerful natural language understanding and generation, allowing users to query complex data in plain English.

Multimodal Vision

Integrating multimodal vision capabilities to enable the AI to "see" and interpret visual data from satellites, identifying patterns, objects, and changes over time.

ISRO EO Data Extensibility

Building a system designed to connect with ISRO's data portals, enabling analysis of high-resolution imagery from satellites like Cartosat and Resourcesat.

Fueled by ISRO's Vision

The system is designed to be compatible with a wide range of data from ISRO's world-class Earth Observation satellite fleet, providing invaluable insights for various sectors.

Cartosat Series

High-resolution cartographic and mapping data for urban planning and infrastructure monitoring.

Resourcesat Series

Multispectral data for agriculture, forestry, and water resource management.

RISAT Series

All-weather, day-and-night radar imaging for disaster management and security.

Oceansat Series

Data for oceanographic research, cyclone forecasting, and marine resource studies.
